### 2. FISH / SHARK / SALMON — Smell Through Water
- **Salmon**: memorize natal stream amino acids during downstream migration

- **Lateral line**: Row of pores from snout to tail detecting pressure changes.

## How Smell Moves: Air vs. Water vs. Digital

### In Air (terrestrial organisms)
- **Diffusion + convection**: molecules evaporate, carried by wind
- **Turbulent plumes**: not smooth gradients but intermittent patches
  of varying concentration
- Speed depends on: molecular weight, temperature, humidity, wind
- Dogs follow turbulent plumes using temporal correlation
- Moths follow plume frequency, not concentration

### In Water (aquatic organisms)
- **Diffusion 10,000× slower** than in air
- But dissolved chemicals travel with currents across vast distances
- Fish smell water entering nostrils directly
- Sharks create pressure maps with lateral line
- Salmon navigate by dissolved amino acid memory

### In Digital (Gaia)
- **Volatile signals**: content emissions, page generation, gallery updates
- **Odor plumes**: human behavior patterns (pages, clicks, dwell, scroll)
- **Turbulent**: not smooth traffic — intermittent bursts of activity
- **Diffusion medium**: HTTP, DNS, social media, search engines, word-of-mouth
- Speed depends on: content freshness, SEO, link topology, engagement rate
