# 📌 PIN THEORY — DISTANCE • DIMENSION • DISPOSITION

## The Idea

One pin is a dot. Nothing.  
Two pins is a line. Distance.  
Three pins is a plane. Dimension.  
Four+ pins is a space. Disposition.  

**Null-to-null = nothing.** No distance, no dimension, no disposition. There is no there there.  
**Pin-to-pin = EVERYTHING.** Distance + dimension + disposition = REAL.

The FEELING of air is null. The REALITY of air is pinned (molecules).  
A rock has distance, dimension, and disposition.  
Air FEELS like it doesn't, but air IS molecules — and molecules are pinned.

**Everything needs pins.**
