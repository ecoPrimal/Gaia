## I. The Orrery — The Mechanical Lens

An orrery is a clockwork mechanical model of the heliocentric Solar System.
The first modern orrery was built by George Graham and Thomas Tompion
(London, ~1711–1712). Named after Charles Boyle, 4th Earl of Orrery.
The Antikythera mechanism (~100 BCE) is the ancient ancestor.

### Properties of the Orrery
- **Not to scale** — but preserves **relative orbital periods**
- Uses circular orbits (not Keplerian ellipses — too hard mechanically)
- Shows the view **from the Sun**, not from Earth
- Gears within gears: nested mechanical relationships
- A candle at center represents the Sun's light

### The Orrery as Lens
The orrery IS a mechanical lens — it takes the invisible relationships
between celestial bodies and makes them visible through clockwork.

**Gaia parallel**: The DAG (738 edges, 138 nodes) IS an orrery. A mechanical
