## The Spring Mechanism

### Three Components

| Part | Material | Color | Location | Physics | Role |
|------|----------|-------|----------|---------|------|
| **Elastic** | Living tissue (tendon, muscle) | Pink/flesh | LEFT | PE = ½kx² | Stores tension from each LEFT pull |
| **Gum** | Klein medial barrier | Bubblegum pink | CENTER | Selectivity = 1/π | Deforms but never breaks |
| **Spring** | Collapsed golden spiral (iron) | Black iron | RIGHT | KE = ½mv² | Launches when released |

### The Golden Spiral Transformation

| Step | State | Dimension | What Happens |
|------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Golden spiral | 2D flat | r = ae^(bθ), grows by φ per quarter turn |
| 2 | Collapse | 2D → 1D coil | Wrap flat spiral around cylinder |
| 3 | Invert | Klein twist | Inside becomes outside (inner → outer) |
| 4 | THE SPRING | 1D coiled + inverted | Iron mountain from the edge |

### The Mathematics

**Golden spiral:**
$$r = ae^{b\theta} \quad \text{where} \quad b = \frac{\ln(\varphi)}{\pi/2}$$

**Collapsed to coil:**
The radius at each coil $n$ follows: $r_n = r_0 \cdot \varphi^{n/4}$

After Klein inversion: $r_n' = r_{\text{max}} - r_n$ (inside becomes outside)

**Spring constant:** Related to the golden ratio:
$$k = \frac{E \cdot \pi}{45} = \frac{102.60 \times 3.14159}{45} = 7.16$$

### Iron Mountain From the Edge

The outer coils of the spring have MORE material because:
- In the original golden spiral, radius increases outward by φ each quarter turn
- When collapsed into a coil, the outer rings are LARGER
- When inverted (Klein), these large outer rings become the EDGE
- The edge has the most iron = "iron mountain from the edge"
